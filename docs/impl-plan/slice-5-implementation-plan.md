# Slice 5 implementation plan

HR and the assigned panelists can take a confirmed candidate through two manual interviews to a recorded decision. Closing a round still revokes candidate access and hands the attempt to the panelist for feedback. An L1 pass enables L2, an incomplete attempt can be rebooked at the same level, and a correction preserves what was originally submitted. Prove the complete workflow on the local stack with synthetic users and AI unavailable. [Requirements roadmap](../core/requirements-roadmap.md#5-complete-l1-and-l2-manually) · [Product plan](../core/product-plan.md#assessments-and-outcomes)

## Existing code and implementation decisions

| Area | What we can reuse | What Slice 5 adds |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Reviewed criteria | Drive skills are ordered, identified by stable UUIDs, and revisioned per drive and level. Editing clears review. | Copy the reviewed criteria into each attempt. Use that copy for validation and display. |
| Eligibility and history | The booking write and the HR queue currently check L1 independently. An awaiting-feedback attempt still occupies the unresolved slot. | Share eligibility rules across queue and command. Add **Feedback submitted** as an attempt state that releases the slot and retains the attempt in History. |
| Booking and access | Existing booking checks candidate and preference revisions, panelist role, time, and overlaps. Invitation, challenge, session, and attendance records are attempt-scoped. | Use the same commands for L2 and rebooks. Bind L2 to the qualifying L1, and reuse revocation for L1 corrections. |
| Staff workspace | HR has **Interviews** with cached lists and booking sheets. Assigned panelists have **My interviews** with a polled round detail. | Add the manual assessment to the panelist detail, extend the queue and booking sheet for L2, and give HR a review inbox and resolution screen. |

- **Freeze criteria at booking for new attempts.** This lets HR fix missing or unreviewed skills before sending an invitation and lets the candidate start on time if HR edits the drive later. Preserve the snapshot on reschedule, link replacement, and reassignment. A fresh attempt takes its own snapshot. Show a link to the drive’s skills when review is needed. Send the displayed skill revision and recheck it and review status in the booking transaction. On a stale request, show the latest reviewed criteria or the action HR needs to take, and preserve the booking draft. This is the proposed timing for Slice 5.
- **Preserve Slice 4 bookings at upgrade.** Mark existing scheduled, active, and awaiting-feedback L1 rounds as eligible for one-time criteria initialization. Leave their timing, assignment, admission, and closure paths available. When an assigned panelist first opens an assessment in **Active** or **Awaiting feedback**, initialize it from the reviewed set used by the first successful initialization command. Show that the criteria were captured after booking on these attempts. If the drive needs a reviewed set, show the required HR action and let interview lifecycle actions continue. Freeze the snapshot before accepting any assessment draft. Historical cancelled, no-show, and withdrawn attempts remain scheduling history.
- **Keep raw notes and questions private to the assigned panelist.** The submitted scorecard contains ratings, optional per-skill feedback, and decision rationale. Those fields become the L1 handoff and the authorized HR readback. Questions and private notes persist with their attempt, including after cancellation, for that panelist to read.
- **Use an in-app HR notification.** Show a durable item in **Needs review** for a correction that reverses an L1 pass with an existing L2. Show a durable informational item when a pass-preserving correction updates the feedback for an existing L2. The review stays actionable until an HR user resolves it. Informational items can be acknowledged by the shared HR team. Record the acknowledging actor and time. The notification links to the affected candidacy and identifies the change.
- **Expose the actions needed to finish the manual flow.** HR can read submitted scorecards when coordinating an L2 or resolving a review. Cross-drive reporting, director dashboards, and outcome totals follow in Slice 6. Live editing and WebSocket closure follow in Slice 7. AI preparation and transcription follow in Slices 9 and 10.

## Decisions, eligibility, and correction rules

The candidacy belongs to one drive. A matching email in another drive can cause a scheduling overlap warning and has independent eligibility, attempts, and decisions. Treat the attempt’s lifecycle, its submitted decision, and the candidacy’s effective outcome as separate data.

Evaluate withdrawal first, then open HR review and any unresolved round. These states block a new booking. With those gates clear, use the controlling submitted L1 and current L2 decisions to expose the next eligible round or closed outcome. Keep a candidacy visible with its next round and a reason when the only booking prerequisite left is reviewed drive skills.

| Committed event | Attempt result | Effect on candidacy |
| --- | --- | --- |
| End either round | **Awaiting feedback** and candidate access revoked | Eligible for panelist submission. Still occupies the unresolved slot. |
| Submit L1 **Pass** | **Feedback submitted** | **Ready for L2**. HR can book a new L2. |
| Submit L1 **Do not proceed** | **Feedback submitted** | Closed for this drive. |
| Submit **Incomplete** at either level | **Feedback submitted**, with a rationale explaining why the attempt was incomplete | Rebook the same level. Prior work stays in history and there is no selection outcome. L2 still requires an effective L1 pass. |
| Submit L2 **Selected** or **Not selected** | **Feedback submitted** | Record the effective hiring decision and close the candidacy. |
| Cancel or mark no-show | Scheduling outcome retained | Rebook the level if the candidacy is still eligible. |
| Withdraw | Candidacy withdrawal retained. Revoke access for a scheduled or active round. | End scheduling eligibility. Keep submitted feedback and withdrawal history. |

Allow at most one unresolved round across both levels per candidacy. For a booking, require a confirmed, eligible candidacy, a reviewed skill set for the next level, and the expected candidacy and workflow revisions under lock. Reject L2 before a submitted L1 pass or during review, reject an L1 booking when the next round is L2, and reject duplicate book requests with different command IDs after the first commits. Distinguish cancellation and no-show from rejection and incompletion in queries and labels.

Apply the existing withdrawal actions to bookable candidacies and scheduled or active attempts. Withdrawal takes precedence over prior assessment state. Retire current L2 authorization and keep the feedback history read-only. A pending review must be resolved before another HR candidacy action, and closed outcomes have read-only scheduling history. [Slice 4 scheduling rules](slice-4-implementation-plan.md#round-states-and-access-changes)

Provide a narrow recovery action for **Awaiting feedback** when the assigned panelist is no longer eligible. HR can cancel the stranded attempt with a reason and schedule a fresh attempt at the same level. Recheck the role and lack of a submitted scorecard in the transaction. Preserve private work and attendance, mark the cancellation as panelist unavailable, and record the HR actor and reason. HR can use the existing close-round action first if the round is still active. The replacement panelist assesses the fresh attempt.

### Corrections before and after L2

- Corrections append a new immutable version with the previous version ID, author, time, and a required reason. Let the currently assigned panelist correct the controlling submitted attempt while it remains the latest booked attempt at that level. Keep superseded and HR-cancelled attempts readable as history for their authorized viewers. This protects a newer same-level rebook from a late change to an older one. Guard all L2 corrections during an open HR review, and keep all assessment versions read-only after withdrawal.
- For a correction with no L2 in the current L1 lineage, use the new L1 decision to determine the next action. For L1 **Pass** to **Pass** with an existing L2, keep the L2 and its invitation. A current **Scheduled**, **Active**, or **Awaiting feedback** L2 can read the latest passing scorecard in its qualifying lineage. During first L2 submission, compare the L1 version acknowledged by the panelist with the current passing version and require review of a stale handoff. Store the L1 version the L2 panelist used on the submitted L2 version. A submitted L2 and each of its historical versions can read that exact pinned L1 version, including during review. An L2 correction carries forward the L1 reference from its prior submitted version. An inactive L2 with no submission has no L1 handoff. Apply current L2 assignment checks to all these reads.
- When an L1 correction takes away a pass and the current lineage has an L2, append the L1 version and open a review in the same transaction. Put the candidacy in **Pending HR review** and suppress the effective outcome. Retain the affected L2 attempt and the submitted version eligible for reinstatement, if one exists. Cancel an unfinished L2 in **Scheduled**, **Active**, or **Awaiting feedback**, revoke its invitation, challenges, and sessions, and keep its attendance and draft. Record a structured cancellation reason and timestamp, and retain the original interview close time if it was already recorded. Preserve a submitted L2 as history. Reject further L2 booking, admission, draft saves, submissions, and corrections while review is open.
- An L1 panelist can correct again while a review is open. Use one open review per candidacy. Preserve the originally affected L2 and every intervening L1 version. Keep the review pending when a pass is restored. HR must resolve against the latest L1 version and review revision displayed.

| Latest L1 at HR resolution | Available resolution |
| --- | --- |
| **Do not proceed** | Apply the decision, clear the current L2 pointer, and close the candidacy. The old L2 remains history. |
| **Incomplete** | Apply the decision, clear the current L2 pointer, and enable a fresh L1. The old L2 remains history. |
| **Pass**, affected L2 had a submitted decision | Reinstate the captured L2 version. A reinstated **Incomplete** enables a fresh L2. A reinstated selection decision restores that effective outcome. |
| **Pass**, affected L2 was unfinished or already ended without a submission | Clear the current L2 pointer and enable a fresh L2 with a new invitation. Retain the old attempt and its work. |

Use the current L2 pointer to find the affected attempt. If an incomplete L2 was followed by an unfinished L2 rebook, the unfinished rebook is the affected attempt. If HR has already resolved a review by applying loss of the pass, a later eligible L1 correction to **Pass** can open fresh L2 scheduling. A reversal before that fresh booking has no current L2 to review. A reversal after a fresh booking targets the new L2. Reinstating an old L2 belongs to resolution of its open review. HR records who resolved it, when, why, the L1 version applied, and any reinstated L2 version. The HR command applies a panelist decision. Only the assigned panelist authors or corrects a decision. ## Data, authorization, and transactions

Use additive Drizzle migrations and shared Zod contracts. Keep the new assessment writes in the interviews module. Route handlers authenticate, parse, and call the module. Put eligibility, transitions, review, and revocation checks where both the normal UI and direct API calls use them.

| Record | Required fields and invariants |
| --- | --- |
| Attempt assessment | One per round with frozen skill IDs and labels in display order, original reviewed skill revision and review time, snapshot time, and an upgrade marker for legacy initialization. Persist draft notes, stable question IDs and text, partial ratings and feedback, optional decision and rationale, revision, and updated time. Keep version history separate from the editable draft. |
| Submitted assessment version | Stable ID, attempt ID, unique version number per attempt, prior version, outcome, ratings and optional skill feedback, rationale, author, and timestamp. Require a reason for corrections. Store the reviewed L1 version for L2. Submission and correction are append-only. |
| Candidacy assessment progress | One row per drive and candidate. Track the effective L1 version, the current L2 attempt if any, and a revision. Store the qualifying L1 attempt and booking-time pass version on L2. Derive next action and effective outcome from these references, current round states, withdrawal, and open review. A new L2 rebook becomes current; older attempts stay in history. Suppress effective outcome during review. |
| HR review and notification | Scope by candidacy. Keep one open review with the trigger L1 version, affected L2 and any reinstatable submitted version, and its revision. Retain resolution actor, time, reason, and applied IDs. Persist notification events with the related submission version and their read status. Use IDs and state codes in audit events. |

- Add **Feedback submitted** to the state contract, database enum, and staff history reads. Keep admission limited to scheduled or active rounds. Update the partial unique index from one unresolved round per level to one per candidacy. Check existing data before changing the index and exercise the migration on a database seeded at Slice 4.
- Scope progress, review, and version references to the same candidacy and correct round level. Use foreign keys and uniqueness constraints where those invariants fit. Check state and version ownership in the write transaction. Use deterministic paging and a shared eligibility projection for list reads and the locked booking decision. Treat a missing progress record as revision zero and create it on the first workflow write under the candidacy lock. Advance the workflow revision with bookings, cancellation, no-show, withdrawal, submission, correction, and HR resolution. Return server-derived available actions and the workflow revision to the UI. A candidate close changes the attempt revision and keeps the unresolved slot occupied.
- Serialize booking, first submit, correction, and HR resolution on the existing candidacy row. Use the existing receipt before domain locks on staff writes, then candidacy, the reviewed skill-set row when taking a snapshot, required staff and scheduling resource locks, affected round rows in stable ID order, and dependent assessment and admission records. Preserve the candidacy's `FOR NO KEY UPDATE` lock for candidate-side foreign-key checks. Lock the skills before staff rows for both booking and legacy initialization. The drive review write can already hold the skill-set row while its reviewer foreign key locks a staff row. Use a consistent ID order if locking multiple staff rows. Recheck the latest state and revisions after acquiring the required locks.
- Preserve the candidate path’s round-first locking. Before changing an affected L2, the correction holds that round lock, updates the state, and runs the existing admission revocation in the same transaction as the review write. A join or heartbeat that completes before the correction may succeed; subsequent access sees revocation. Keep candidate handlers from taking a candidacy write lock after locking a round. Preserve the generic acknowledgment for an exact retry of an already-committed candidate close.
- Use distinct expected revisions for draft edits, submission, corrections, and review resolution. A submit checks the latest draft and attempt state. A correction checks the submitted head and current eligibility. An HR resolution checks the review and the latest L1 version. Exact retries return the committed resource through the existing command receipt pattern after checking the current caller’s authorization. A changed request requires a new command ID. Conflicts return the latest authorized state and keep the UI draft available.
- Enforce current role and assignment on every request, including version reads, stale responses, draft saves, and command replay. Use dedicated DTOs for own draft, submitted scorecard, HR review, and L2 handoff. Derive the L1 handoff from the assignee’s L2 round and its qualifying lineage. Keep assessment fields out of candidate DTOs, runtime logs, and URLs. Keep draft content out of HR lists and the L2 handoff. ## HTTP interfaces

Use the existing `/api/staff` authentication, origin checks, no-store responses, and typed errors. Keep assessment draft routes under the assigned-round resource and HR review routes under the HR workspace. These paths describe the intended interfaces.

| Endpoint under `/api/staff` | Interface |
| --- | --- |
| `GET /my-interviews/:roundId/assessment` | Own criteria, draft, current version, permitted actions, and read-only L1 feedback when applicable. Return an initialization state for legacy rounds. |
| `POST /my-interviews/:roundId/assessment/initialize` | Idempotently freeze reviewed criteria for a legacy attempt. A read can then return the saved snapshot. |
| `PATCH /my-interviews/:roundId/assessment/draft` | Save the revisioned manual draft. Limit draft edits to active or awaiting-feedback rounds. |
| `POST /my-interviews/:roundId/assessment/submit` | Submit the saved draft with expected revisions and command ID. Create the first submitted version, make the private notes and questions read-only, and update attempt and progress atomically. |
| `POST /my-interviews/:roundId/assessment/correct` | Submit a full replacement scorecard and correction reason, with the expected head version and command ID. Preserve every prior version and the private notes and questions. |
| `GET /interviews`, `POST /interviews` | Extend the existing list and booking contracts for level, eligible next action, workflow revision, and reviewed-criteria status. Keep existing checks for profile, preferences, role, slot, and overlaps. |
| `GET /interviews/:roundId/assessment` | HR can read the current submitted version for workflow coordination. Include status and relevant audit metadata. |
| `GET …/:roundId/assessment/versions`, `GET …/:roundId/assessment/versions/:versionId` | List version metadata with pagination and fetch one full version on demand. Use the same authorization and response shapes as the corresponding assigned panelist or HR resource. |
| `GET /interviews/reviews`, `GET /interviews/reviews/:reviewId` | Paginated inbox and scoped review detail with the versions and resolution options HR needs. |
| `POST /interviews/reviews/:reviewId/resolve` | Record a reason and apply the submitted choice against current review and L1 revisions. |

Register literal routes before parameterized routes where they could overlap. Add an idempotent read acknowledgment for informational inbox items. Validate skill IDs against the snapshot, uniqueness of ratings and questions, level-specific outcomes, and trimmed required reasons on the server. Let drafts be partial. Submitted versions carry all skill ratings, including explicit **Not assessed**. Apply an assessment-specific payload limit without the existing 4 KiB scheduling middleware rejecting larger notes first. As initial bounds, support up to 50 questions of 500 characters, 16,000 characters of private notes, 500 characters of feedback per frozen skill, and 2,000 characters each for rationale and correction reason. Enforce an overall byte limit sized for those bounds and multibyte input. Test the maximum valid requests and oversized payloads. Use the staff error responses for inaccessible assignments or round IDs and stable conflict codes for stale versions, missing skill review, unavailable actions, and pending review. ## Build sequence

1. **Manual L1.** Add the migration, contracts, and transaction tests. Implement criteria capture, legacy initialization, and persisted questions, notes, and ratings. Update seed fixtures and Slice 4 regression tests to supply reviewed skills when the new booking checks require them. Wire the assigned panelist screen through closing and submitting L1. Verify approval, rejection, incomplete rebooking, and authorization on local synthetic rounds.
2. **L2 scheduling and decisions.** Extend both HR eligibility surfaces and the existing booking command. Exercise the same invitation and candidate lifecycle for L2, expose the authorized L1 handoff, and submit a final or incomplete L2. Add reviewed L2 skills to the two-round fixtures.
3. **Corrections and HR resolution.** Add versioned corrections, the inbox and review decision screen, atomic L2 cancellation and revocation, and reinstatement or rebooking. Treat the first two passes as local milestones. Complete this pass before Slice 5 acceptance.
4. **Rehearse and review.** Run `pnpm check`, `pnpm test` with isolated PostgreSQL, and `pnpm test:browser` with a fresh isolated Compose project and Mailpit. Test the pre-Slice-5 migration and repeat seeding. Exercise the panelist and HR workspaces at desktop and narrow widths. Review the draft, feedback submission, L2 handoff, and HR review before declaring the slice complete. [Local verification](../../README.md#local-interview-rehearsal)

## Acceptance matrix

| Scenario | Required evidence |
| --- | --- |
| Manual path | HR books L1, the candidate joins, the panelist closes and submits **Pass**, HR books L2, its assignee reads L1 and submits **Selected**. A different L2 candidate can receive **Not selected**. Run with AI unavailable. Verify two distinct attempt IDs and invitations. |
| Exit and rebook | L1 **Do not proceed** leaves the scheduling queue for that drive. **Incomplete** at each level permits a same-level rebook, creates no selection outcome, and retains old notes and versions. Cancellation, no-show, and withdrawal keep their scheduling semantics. |
| Snapshot and upgrade | Change drive skills after booking and race a skills save or review against booking and legacy initialization. Each successful snapshot contains one reviewed revision and retains its original IDs, order, and labels. A later attempt uses the newly reviewed set. An upgraded scheduled, active, or awaiting-feedback round can complete. With missing skills it can still close and its assessment can resume after HR reviews the skills. |
| Access and data scope | An unrelated or reassigned panelist cannot read or mutate the assessment through either screen or direct request. Candidate sessions cannot access staff feedback. The L2 assignee sees only submitted L1 feedback for the correct drive. Check live, submitted, cancelled, reinstated, and retired L2 handoff reads. Same-email candidacies in different drives stay independent. |
| Panelist becomes unavailable | Change the assigned role after the interview starts and after closure. Verify HR can end an active round and cancel a stranded awaiting-feedback attempt with an audit reason. A fresh same-level booking can proceed. Verify the old draft is retained, HR cannot submit a scorecard, and the recovery action is rejected for an eligible assignee or a submitted attempt. |
| L1 pass reversed during L2 | Run against a scheduled round with an admitted candidate, an active L2, and a closed L2 awaiting feedback. Verify the old link, any pre-existing code, and admitted session are denied, the unfinished L2 is cancelled, and any acknowledged draft is retained. Verify HR sees the review and L2 writes and new booking are blocked. |
| L1 pass reversed after L2 submission | Reverse after **Selected**. Verify no effective outcome during review and both scorecards still exist. Resolve once for loss of pass, then separately restore a pass and reinstate a prior decision. Exercise reinstating an **Incomplete** and rebooking at L2. Exercise the fresh-L2 path when the affected attempt was unfinished. |
| Multiple cycles and withdrawal | Apply loss of pass, correct L1 to **Pass**, and reverse again before a fresh L2 booking. The retired L2 remains history. Repeat after a fresh L2 to verify the new attempt is affected. Withdraw a candidacy with prior feedback and verify historical permissions and denial of further progression and correction. |
| Correction and concurrency | Check pass-preserving L1 corrections and the informational HR notification. Check a stale L1 handoff at L2 submit, a stale HR resolution after another L1 correction, booking versus reversal, candidate admission versus cancellation, L2 submission versus reversal, and competing HR resolves. Use actual database transactions. Verify exact retries create one version, review, or booking and overridden scheduling conflicts remain advisory. |
| UI recovery | Save and reload a draft. Simulate a lost submit response and retry. Keep draft text on a stale revision and prevent delayed completions from replacing another sheet. Verify keyboard focus and large valid Unicode inputs. Check that authorized cached screens pick up status changes from a second user. |

For races, force both lock acquisition orders. If L2 booking or admission commits first, the correction revokes it. If L2 submission commits first, retain its submitted scorecard and suppress the outcome when review opens. If the correction commits first, those L2 operations are denied. A single current outcome and one open review per candidacy are invariants across these cases.
